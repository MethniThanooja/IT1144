About University of Vavuniya

The University of Vavuniya is a public university located in Vavuniya District, Sri Lanka. Established in June 2021, it was formed by converting the Vavuniya Campus of the University of Jaffna into an independent university.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Information Technology, Business Studies, Applied Science, and other disciplines. It focuses on practical education, research, and regional development.
